**Velvet Vault** — a top-down stealth heist in three rooms. Sneak past patrolling guards and their vision cones, dodge sweeping cameras, lift the loot off its pedestals, and slip out through the getaway door before the alarm meter fills. Guards get suspicious, then chase; at full alarm the whole room goes into lockdown. Tools help at a cost: decoy coins (Q / right-click, -$150) pull guards off their routes, and an EMP pulse (F, -$300) blinds every camera for six seconds. Sneaking (Shift) is slower but halves how fast cones fill the alarm and keeps your footsteps silent. Clear all three rooms — Lobby of Lamps, Gem Hall, and The Velvet Vault itself — for the clean getaway. Ghost bonuses reward never being seen.

Controls: WASD / arrow keys or hold the mouse to move · Shift to sneak · hold E next to loot to lift it · Q or right-click for a decoy · F for EMP · P to pause · M for sound · R to restart. Vanilla JavaScript and canvas, no build step, no network requests: every asset is drawn or synthesised at runtime (WebAudio), so the game plays fully offline from `game/index.html`.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome
```
